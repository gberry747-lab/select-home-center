Manufactured Home Notes

  • Office publishes a downloadable Mobile Home Permit application and handles building permits, zoning, and code enforcement in one office.
  • County also posts its Zoning Ordinance and Flood Prevention Ordinance on the Codes & Ordinances page of the county site.

Georgia installations must follow the state Rules for Manufactured Homes (O.C.G.A. Title 8-2) using state-licensed installers; a state installation permit and insignia are required, plus the county permits above.
