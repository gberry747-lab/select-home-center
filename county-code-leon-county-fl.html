Health Department (Septic & Well Permits)

Septic and well approvals run through the health department - most counties require the septic permit or existing-system approval before the move-on permit is issued.

Department: Florida Department of Health in Leon County

Address: Tallahassee, FL 32301

Phone: 850-404-6300

Environmental Health (septic/well): 850-895-8360 (EH: 435 North Macomb St., Tallahassee)

Website:
https://leon.floridahealth.gov

Manufactured home requirements:

  • Septic permits for this county are issued DIRECTLY by Florida DEP - apply at OSTDS.FloridaDEP.gov (DEP: 850-245-4070); the health department helps with records and questions

Manufactured Home Notes

  • All residential permit applications, fees, and inspections are administered at the 435 N. Macomb Street office.
  • Leon County and the City of Tallahassee share a joint online permitting hub (tlcpermits.org) for applications and inspection scheduling.

Florida installations follow FS 320 and Rule 15C-1/15C-2 (DHSMV) with licensed installers and installation decals; county building permits, tie-downs and wind-zone compliance apply.
