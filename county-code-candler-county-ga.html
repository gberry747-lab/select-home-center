Manufactured Home Notes

  • County adopted a Manufactured Home Ordinance and a Manufactured Home & Tiny Home Park Ordinance (April 2024), plus a countywide Zoning Ordinance adopted Sept 23, 2024; ordinances are posted on the county site, not Municode
  • Ordinance and permit questions go through the Board of Commissioners office at 912-685-2835
  • Farm-worker seasonal manufactured homes limited to one per 3 acres with 200-ft separation between homes

Georgia installations must follow the state Rules for Manufactured Homes (O.C.G.A. Title 8-2) using state-licensed installers; a state installation permit and insignia are required, plus the county permits above.
